for the one who made the cold bloom
I
❧
❧
✿ ❀
🎃
For you,

I may only be a poet, a pair of hands that bloom ink instead of holding warmth, a mouth that learned to bleed language instead of saying stay. But for you I’d write on forgetting things: teach water your name until it drowns in itself, stitch syllables into the wind and call it a body. Let me press this love into places that refuse to keep me. a shadow, and I’ll raise it into a skyline, a map without oceans, and I’ll drag a ship across its bones, a pen with no pulse, and I’ll still force it to confess you.

II
❧
❧
✿ ❀

If that’s not enough. then listen. listen to how silence bends, how it breaks and mends, how every unsaid thing learns the shape of a hymn, until even the quiet starts to form you: into something you can finally hear.

III
❧
❧
✿ ❀
🎃

Bestow upon me an eternity of frozen grounds, A breeze that chills no more than death, Or dim, dense clouds that shut away The faintest drop of warmth. A world that bled in blue and white, Left bruised beneath a lightless sky. Its heart confides within the darkness, Now resting in the palms of youth, Where the harshest glaciers thaw. With every breath you take, Comes a new, rejuvenating wind. You shatter the angry clouds, And beneath their wreckage, light leaks through. And it shines upon you.

IV
❧
❧
✿ ❀
❀

At last, my eyes are graced by your beauty; The air itself feeds me a share of life, And my heart begins to beat along with yours. Although you did nothing but exist, You somehow kept the flower blooming— Even after it was plucked From the very earth that gave it life.

- perry
